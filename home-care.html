FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What types of in-home nursing are offered?

Skilled nursing visits, wound care, IV therapy, injections, vitals monitoring and chronic condition management, all by licensed nurses.

How are medication reminders managed?

Your caregiver gives reminders at each dose and logs it in the family portal, so everyone can see what was taken and when.

What chronic illnesses do you support?

Diabetes, hypertension, heart disease, COPD, arthritis, dementia and more, with a care plan built with your doctor.

What personal care assistance is provided?

Bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting, mobility help and fall prevention, always with dignity and respect.

How do you support elderly individuals at home?

Companionship, safety check-ins, meal help, light housekeeping, appointment escorts and memory support.

What therapies can be done at home?

Physical, occupational and speech therapy sessions can all be scheduled at home with licensed therapists.

How does the daily check-in system work?

After each visit, the caregiver records vitals, meals, mood and notes. Family members get an update in the app the same day.

Can family members access the care logs?

Yes. Invite as many family members as you like to the secure family portal to view logs and message the care team.

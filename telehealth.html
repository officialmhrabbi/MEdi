Types of care

Everything your doctor's office does, without the waiting room

From a yearly check-up to managing a long-term condition, see the right clinician the way that suits you.

  • Primary care

    General check-ups and family medicine for every age, with a doctor who knows your history.

  • Telehealth consultations

    Video visits with doctors and specialists, usually within minutes.

  • Prescriptions & medication

    Digital prescriptions, refills and medication reviews sent straight to your pharmacy.

  • Urgent care

    Fast treatment for non-emergency illness and injuries, the same day.

  • Chronic condition care

    Ongoing support for diabetes, hypertension, asthma and more, with regular check-ins.

  • Preventive health & vaccines

    Wellness checks, screenings and immunizations to keep the whole family healthy.
